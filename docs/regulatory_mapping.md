# Regulatory Mapping

This repository maps each risk to relevant articles as **best-effort
references**. These mappings are not legal advice and must be reviewed by
qualified counsel for production deployment.

## EU AI Act (Non-Exhaustive)

Examples of risk-to-article mapping used in the catalog:

- **Art. 9**: Risk management system
- **Art. 10**: Data and data governance
- **Art. 12**: Record-keeping
- **Art. 14**: Human oversight
- **Art. 15**: Accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity

## NIS2 Directive (Non-Exhaustive)

Examples of risk-to-article mapping used in the catalog:

- **Art. 21**: Cybersecurity risk management measures
- **Art. 23**: Reporting obligations

## DORA (Optional, Non-Exhaustive)

Examples of risk-to-article mapping used in the catalog:

- **Art. 6**: ICT risk management framework
- **Art. 8**: ICT systems and tools
- **Art. 15**: ICT-related incident reporting

## Mapping Approach

- Each risk includes explicit EU AI Act and/or NIS2 references.
- Risks may map to multiple articles when controls overlap.
- DORA references are optional and included when relevant.
- The catalog can be extended as regulatory interpretation evolves.
